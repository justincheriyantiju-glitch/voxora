Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. VOXORA has no paid tier or subscription.

Which voices can I use?

Phonon includes a built-in default voice and a catalogue of additional voices. Catalogue voices download when first used. The browser engine uses voices installed on your device.

Can I download the audio?

Yes. With the Phonon engine, generated audio can be downloaded as a WAV file. Browser voices cannot be saved through this app.

What is downloaded the first time?

The Phonon model is downloaded the first time you use that engine. Additional catalogue voices may require separate downloads.

Is VOXORA open source?

Yes. The code lives on GitHub, and contributions are welcome.
