Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. VOXORA has no paid tier and no usage limits.

Which voices can I use?

Phonon ships with a built-in default voice plus a catalog of female and male voices; each catalog voice downloads once the first time you use it. The browser engine uses the voices installed on your device, so that list differs by device.

Can I download the audio?

Yes. With the Phonon engine, every result has a Download WAV button. Browser voices cannot be saved.

What is downloaded the first time?

The Phonon model, once (about 65 MB). Each catalog voice is a small extra download the first time it is used. Your browser keeps both, so later visits load from your device.

Is VOXORA open source?

Yes. The code lives on GitHub, and contributions are welcome.
